# Data licences and attribution
- **Proteomes** (not in git; release archive): UniProt Reference Proteomes, release 2026_03, CC BY 4.0
  (https://www.uniprot.org/help/license). Per-file source, checksum and date: `docs/DATA.md`.
- **Species trees**: our binarised versions of published topologies; sources per level in `panels/<P>/levels.tsv`.
- **Reference families**: gene identifiers, classification labels, event placements and short verbatim quotes (≤300
  characters) taken from the publications listed in each `families/<ID>/family.yaml` (`sources`). Credit belongs to
  those authors; cite them when you use a family. Full texts and supplements are not redistributed.
- **COG 2020** membership (K01, K04) was used only to locate the papers' proteins: Galperin et al. 2021, NCBI COG.
- **YGOB** pillars (Y01): Byrne & Wolfe 2005, archived copy (Wayback Machine) of Pillars.tab (truncated) and YGOB v1 tables.
