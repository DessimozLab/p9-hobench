All five defects were found by trivial baselines or by error analysis (E5 and the aggregation defect by error analysis). Each fix changed the definition for every
method in the same way; values before and after are in \texttt{results/ERRATA.md}. E1, E2 and E4 were decided before
results for panels E, V and P were seen. Entry E3 in \texttt{results/ERRATA.md} records reporting changes from design
review round 2 (baselines and skill shown with every endpoint; separate control track), not a defect. E5 was triggered by an error analysis of a FastOMA result on panel P.

\section*{Blind verification}
A verifier agent that had not curated the families re-derived min(all, 30) seeded assertions per family from the stored
sources, without seeing the claimed values. Two or more discrepancies failed a family; one discrepancy triggered a
second sample. Upper bound = two-sided 95\% Clopper--Pearson bound on the discrepancy rate.
